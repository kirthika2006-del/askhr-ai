ACME TECHNOLOGIES — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SAMPLE)

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued at 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum
of 10 days into the next calendar year. Annual leave requests must be submitted at least
5 working days in advance through the HR portal and require manager approval.

1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. For sick leave exceeding
3 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be submitted to HR. Sick leave does not
carry forward to the next year.

1.3 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per statutory
guidelines. Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave,
to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

1.4 Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with prior manager approval. Fully
remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly.

SECTION 2: ONBOARDING

2.1 First Week Checklist
New employees complete laptop setup, HR documentation, and mandatory compliance training
(Code of Conduct, Information Security, POSH Policy) within their first week.

2.2 Probation Period
All new hires undergo a 90-day probation period, during which performance is reviewed by
the reporting manager at the 30-day and 60-day marks, with a final confirmation review
at day 90.

2.3 Buddy Program
Every new employee is assigned a peer buddy from their team for the first 30 days to help
with onboarding questions and cultural orientation.

SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT

3.1 Workplace Behavior
Employees are expected to maintain professional conduct at all times. Harassment,
discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and can be reported
confidentially through the Ethics Hotline or directly to HR.

3.2 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interests, or relationships
that could reasonably create a conflict of interest with their role at Acme Technologies.

3.3 Confidentiality
All employees are bound by the Confidentiality and IP Assignment Agreement signed at the
time of joining. Company data, source code, and client information must not be shared
outside the organization without written authorization.

SECTION 4: BENEFITS

4.1 Health Insurance
Acme Technologies provides group health insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up
to 2 dependent children, with a sum insured of ₹5,00,000 per family per year.

4.2 Provident Fund
Employer and employee both contribute 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident
Fund as per statutory requirements.

4.3 Wellness Allowance
Employees receive an annual wellness allowance of ₹10,000, reimbursable against gym
memberships, fitness classes, or mental health counseling sessions.

SECTION 5: EXIT POLICY

5.1 Notice Period
The standard notice period is 60 days for all confirmed employees. Employees on
probation must serve a 15-day notice period.

5.2 Full and Final Settlement
Full and final settlement, including any pending reimbursements and leave encashment,
is processed within 45 days of the last working day.

5.3 Exit Interview
All departing employees are required to complete an exit interview with HR to share
feedback about their experience at the company.
